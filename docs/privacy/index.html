Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

In short: Murmur does not collect your data. Your recordings and transcripts stay on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Murmur stores the following only on your device, in the app’s own storage:

  • your audio recordings and imported audio
  • transcripts, summaries and action items
  • folders, custom dictionary words and app settings

We have no server and cannot see any of this. Deleting a note in Murmur removes its transcript and audio from the device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Like other app data, it may be included in device backups that you control, such as iCloud or computer backups.

Microphone

Murmur uses the microphone only while you are recording. Audio is processed on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Files and videos you import

When you import a file from Files or a video from Photos, Murmur reads only the items you choose and processes them on your device.

Speech recognition and summaries

Transcription runs on your device using an open speech model (Whisper). Summaries, where available, are created by Apple’s on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). Your audio and text are not sent to us or to any third party for either.

The one download: the speech model

The first time you use Murmur, it downloads the speech model files from an online model-hosting service (currently Hugging Face). Like any download, this reveals your IP address and basic technical information to that service. No recordings, transcripts or personal information are sent. After the model is downloaded, Murmur works without an internet connection. The hosting service’s own privacy policy applies to that download.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and we never receive your payment details. Murmur only learns, on your device, whether you have an active purchase.

Sharing and exporting

If you export or share a transcript, summary or audio file, it goes to the app or person you choose. What happens to it there is up to them.

Children

Murmur does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control everything in the app: delete notes, delete the speech model, or delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/killoldesai/murmur/issues. Please do not post personal information there.
